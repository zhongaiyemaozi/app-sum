隐私政策

禅音木鱼把敲击计数、练习历史，以及音色、样式、节奏和每日目标保存在这台设备上，用来完成本机计数和展示。

1. 不收集的内容

不要求注册或登录。不收集设备标识、通讯录、精确位置或生物识别信息。没有广告或追踪 SDK，也没有开发者服务器。

2. 购买

Pro 是可选的一次性买断，由 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理付款和恢复购买。开发者收不到银行卡信息。

3. iCloud

Pro 可以把计数和偏好备份到你自己的 iCloud 键值存储。该数据属于你的 Apple 账户，开发者不能读取。未开启时数据只留在本机。

4. 删除

你可以在应用内重置计数。卸载应用会删除本机数据。iCloud 中的键值由系统设置里的 iCloud 管理。

Privacy policy

Wooden Fish stores tap counts, practice history, and your sound, style, rhythm, and daily goal on this device.

1. What is not collected

There is no account. The app does not collect a device identifier, contacts, precise location, or biometric data. It has no advertising or tracking SDK and no developer server.

2. Purchases

Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Apple StoreKit processes payment and restore. The developer does not receive card details.

3. iCloud

Pro can back up counts and preferences to your own iCloud key-value storage. That data belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot read it. Until you turn backup on, data stays on this device.

4. Deletion

You can reset counts in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data. iCloud key-value data is managed in system iCloud settings.
