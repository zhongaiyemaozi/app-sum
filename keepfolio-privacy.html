隐私政策

物有凭把家电和数码产品的保修资料保存在本机，包括你添加的照片和 PDF。

1. 本机数据

记录、房间、保养周期和附件默认只在这台设备上。条码在本机解码，不联网查询商品库。没有账号，也没有开发者服务器。

2. 可选 iCloud

iCloud 同步使用你同一 Apple ID 的私有数据库。它默认关闭，不是备份。关闭同步不会删除这台设备上的记录。开发者不能读取这些数据。

3. 购买与分享

Pro 由 Apple StoreKit 处理。你主动导出或分享的资料包由系统分享面板交给你选择的对象。

Privacy policy

Keepfolio stores warranty records for appliances and devices on this iPhone or iPad, including photos and PDFs you add.

1. On this device

Records, rooms, service intervals, and attachments stay on the device by default. Barcodes are decoded on the device and are not looked up online. There is no account and no developer server.

2. Optional iCloud

iCloud sync uses the private database of the same Apple ID. It is off until you turn it on, and it is not a backup. Turning it off does not delete records on this device. The developer cannot read the data.

3. Purchases and sharing

Apple StoreKit handles Pro. A packet you export or share goes through the system share sheet to the person you choose.
