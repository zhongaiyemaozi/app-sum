隐私政策

教材下载是个人使用的目录工具，不提供账号和内购。

1. 你会发起的网络请求

浏览教材目录、下载你点选的 PDF，以及用已启用的书源搜索和下载小说时，请求发往对应的 GitHub 仓库或该书源网站。开发者不运营这些站点，也不把教材或小说正文打进应用包。

2. 本机缓存

下载的文件保存在本机缓存，你可以删除。应用不收集姓名、电话或精确位置。

3. 你的责任

只下载你有权保存的文件。需要登录或网页验证的书源不在支持范围内。

Privacy policy

Textbook Downloader is a personal catalog tool. It has no account and no in-app purchase.

1. Network requests you start

Browsing the textbook catalog, downloading a PDF you choose, and searching or downloading novels through a book source you enabled send requests to that GitHub repository or book-source site. The developer does not operate those sites and does not ship textbook or novel text inside the app.

2. Cache

Downloaded files stay in the on-device cache and you can delete them. The app does not collect your name, phone number, or precise location.

3. Your responsibility

Download only files you have a right to keep. Sources that require a login or a web challenge are not supported.
