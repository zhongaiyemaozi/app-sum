Privacy policy

Toolbox is an offline utility for character counts, text cleanup, timestamps, unit conversion, Base64, and QR codes. It has no account and no in-app purchase.

1. What you type

Conversion, encoding, and QR codes stay on the device. The app does not upload the text you enter to the developer or to any other server.

2. What is not collected

The app does not collect your name, phone number, or precise location, and it does not track you.
