隐私政策

青页隐私政策。版本 2.0。更新日期：2026年9月2日。生效日期：2026年7月24日。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于青页提供的本机阅读、书架管理、阅读进度、书签、阅读统计和备份功能。

2. 信息处理

青页不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、手机号、身份证号、精确位置、通讯录或设备标识。仅在你主动选择文件后，在本机处理 TXT / EPUB / Markdown 的内容、文件名、书名、作者及文件大小，用于解析和展示书籍。EPUB 插图、样式与字体只在本机解包，保存在应用私有目录。这些内容不会上传到开发者服务器。

3. 本机存储与期限

书架、阅读进度、书签、阅读统计、阅读设置和你主动创建的备份保存在本设备，直到你在应用内清除、卸载应用或自行删除导出的备份。

4. 系统能力

导入书籍、导入备份或导出备份时，会打开系统文件选择器，只处理你明确选择的文件。听书使用系统语音合成。检查 App Store 是否有新版本时只连接 Apple，不上传书籍内容。

5. 第三方

青页不接入广告、统计或追踪 SDK，不向第三方出售或共享个人信息。你主动使用系统分享时，内容由你选择的目标应用处理。

6. 你的权利

你可以在应用内清除书架、阅读记录、书签、统计与设置，也可以卸载应用。导出的备份由你在系统文件里自行管理。

7. 未成年人

青页不面向未满十四周岁的儿童专门提供服务，也不主动收集儿童个人信息。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，并只导入有权阅读的内容。

8. 政策更新

如果处理目的或范围发生重大变化，会更新政策版本。

Privacy policy

Qingye Privacy Policy. Version 2.0. Updated September 2, 2026. Effective July 24, 2026.

1. Scope

This policy covers on-device reading, the shelf, progress, bookmarks, reading stats, and backups.

2. Information

Qingye does not require an account and does not collect your name, phone number, identification number, precise location, contacts, or a device identifier. After you choose a file, TXT, EPUB, and Markdown content, the file name, title, author, and size are processed on the device to open the book. EPUB images, styles, and fonts are unpacked only on the device into the app's private folder. Book content is not uploaded to the developer.

3. Storage

The shelf, progress, bookmarks, stats, settings, and backups you create stay on the device until you clear them, uninstall the app, or delete an exported backup.

4. System services

Import and backup use the system file picker and only the files you select. Read-aloud uses the system speech synthesizer. An App Store version check contacts Apple only and does not upload books.

5. Third parties

There is no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. The app does not sell or share personal information. System share sends content only to the app you choose.

6. Your choices

You can clear the shelf, progress, bookmarks, stats, and settings in the app, or uninstall it. Exported backups stay in the files you manage.

7. Children

Qingye is not directed at children under 14 and does not seek children's personal information. A minor should use it with a guardian and import only content they may read.

8. Updates

The policy version changes if the purpose or scope of processing changes in a material way.
