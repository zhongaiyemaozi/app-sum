Ugly Avatar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Ugly Avatar draws faces on your device. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics SDK. Your faces and favorites are not uploaded to the developer.

1. What stays on this device

Faces are drawn on this device. Favorites store the seed, the time you saved it, and a PNG in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server we operate.

2. Photos permission

Save asks Photos for permission to add pictures only, for example when you keep a face to use as a contact photo. The app does not read the rest of your library. If you deny access, generation and favorites still work; turn the permission on later in Settings if you want to save.

3. Purchases

Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase handled by Apple. This app stores only a local entitlement flag so Pro can be recognized offline. Restore uses your Apple ID. We never see your payment details.

4. What we do not collect

There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics SDK. The daily free-roll counter lives in this app's settings storage on the device. Version 1.0 does not sync favorites through iCloud.

Terms of Use

1. What the app does

The app rolls procedural faces, lets you nudge proportions, save a picture, share a card, and keep favorites. A seed recreates the same face. Results are generated for fun and are not a portrait of a real person.

2. Free tier and Pro

The free tier includes basic features and a limited number of new rolls each day. Pro is a one-time unlock for the extra feature set, motion, high-resolution export, and unlimited rolls. The price shown at purchase is the price from the App Store. Pro does not renew.

3. Faces you create

Faces you roll belong to you as pictures on your device. Do not use them to imitate a real person in a misleading way. Seeds are just numbers; sharing a seed lets someone else redraw that face.

4. Contact

For privacy, purchase, or support questions, email zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com. If a purchase fails, check the App Store sign-in and try again, or use Restore Purchases.

丑萌头像 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月12日

丑萌头像在你的设备上画脸。没有账号，没有广告，也没有统计 SDK。你的头像和收藏不会上传给开发者。

1. 留在本机的内容

头像在这台设备上绘制。收藏会把种子、收藏时间和一张 PNG 放进本 App 的私有空间。这些内容不会上传到开发者的服务器。

2. 相册权限

点保存时，App 只会向系统申请“添加照片”的权限，例如把一张脸存下来当作联系人头像。它不会读取相册里的其他照片。拒绝后仍然可以生成和收藏；若以后想保存，可在系统设置里打开该权限。

3. 购买

Pro 是由 Apple 处理的一次性非消耗型购买。本 App 只在本地记下权益标记，方便离线识别 Pro。恢复购买走你的 Apple ID。开发者看不到付款信息。

4. 我们不收集的内容

没有账号，没有广告，也没有统计 SDK。每日免费次数记在本机的 App 设置里。1.0 不会通过 iCloud 同步收藏。

使用条款

1. 这个 App 做什么

本 App 用程序生成怪趣头像，并提供比例微调、保存图片、分享卡片和收藏。输入种子会画出同一张脸。结果只为好玩，不是某个真人的肖像。

2. 免费版与 Pro

免费版包含基础五官，并且每天只能新生成有限次数。Pro 是一次性解锁，包含更多五官、动态效果、高清导出和不限次数生成。购买时看到的价格来自 App Store。Pro 不会自动续费。

3. 你生成的头像

你生成的头像作为本机图片归你使用。请不要用它们去误导他人、冒充某个真人。种子只是一串数字；把种子分享出去，对方就能重画出同一张脸。

4. 联系我们

隐私、购买或支持方面的问题，请发送邮件至 zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com。如果购买失败，请检查 App Store 登录后再试，或使用恢复购买。
