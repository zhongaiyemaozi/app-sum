隐私政策

最后更新：2026年5月29日

Sparks 情侣互动游戏（以下简称「本应用」）非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

一、数据收集

本应用不收集任何个人识别信息。所有用户数据（包括游戏记录、心愿清单、每日一问内容）均存储在您的 iCloud 私有数据库中，仅您和您配对的伴侣可以访问。

二、数据存储

用户数据通过 Apple iCloud 服务存储与同步。邀请码临时存储在 iCloud 公共数据库中，使用后 24 小时内自动删除。本应用不使用任何第三方数据分析、追踪或广告服务。本应用不在开发者服务器上存储任何用户数据。

三、数据共享

本应用不向任何第三方出售、出租或分享您的数据。数据仅在您和您的伴侣之间通过 Apple 的加密服务进行同步。

四、所需权限

iCloud 用于数据存储和伴侣同步。推送通知用于伴侣互动提醒，可随时在系统设置中关闭。

五、数据安全

数据传输依托 Apple 的安全基础设施。开发者无法访问您存储在 iCloud 中的内容。

六、账号删除

您可以随时在「我的」页面中删除账号，删除后所有关联数据将被清除且不可恢复。

七、儿童隐私

本应用适用于 17 岁及以上用户，不面向儿童提供服务。

八、政策变更

如本隐私政策发生重大变更，将通过应用内通知告知您。

Privacy policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Sparks, a couple's interaction game, does not collect personal identifiers for its own servers.

1. Data

Game records, wish lists, and daily-question content are stored in your private iCloud database. Only you and the partner you pair with can access them.

2. Storage

Apple iCloud stores and syncs that data. An invite code is stored temporarily in the iCloud public database and deleted within 24 hours after use. The app does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it does not store user data on a developer server.

3. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Sync between you and your partner uses Apple's encrypted services.

4. Permissions

iCloud is used for storage and partner sync. Push notifications are optional reminders and can be turned off in Settings.

5. Security

Transport uses Apple's infrastructure. The developer cannot read what you store in iCloud.

6. Deletion

You can delete the account from the Me page. Associated data is then removed and cannot be restored.

7. Children

The app is for people 17 and older and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

A material change to this policy is shown in the app.
