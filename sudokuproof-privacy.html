隐私政策

摸鱼游戏厅以本地存储为主。除非你开启私有 iCloud 同步或自行分享，否则游戏数据留在设备上。

1. 设备上的数据

题目、当前游戏、候选数、回放、偏好和进度保存在本机，因此离线也能完成整局。

2. 私有 iCloud 同步

iCloud 可用时，Core Data 可把进度同步到你的私有 CloudKit 数据库。开发者不会接收或运营这些数据；同步失败时本地游戏仍可用。

3. Game Center 与购买

通过 Apple StoreKit 验证和恢复 Pro 及历史答案包购买。不含广告或第三方统计 SDK。

4. 控制与支持

你可以关闭 Game Center、无需账号游玩，并在设置中删除本地记录。如对本政策有疑问，请联系支持。

Privacy Policy

Pause Arcade is local-first. Gameplay stays on your device unless you enable private iCloud sync or share something yourself.

1. Data on your device

Puzzles, active games, notes, replays, preferences, and progress are stored locally so the complete game works offline.

2. Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, Core Data may mirror progress to your private CloudKit database. The developer does not receive or operate this data, and local play remains available if sync fails.

3. Game Center and purchases

Apple StoreKit verifies and restores Pro and previously purchased answer packs. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is included.

4. Control and support

You can keep Game Center off, play without an account, and delete local records from Settings. Contact support for questions about this policy.

Terms of Use

Play fairly, use the app lawfully, and take breaks that suit you.

1. Puzzle service

Sudoku Proof provides offline logic puzzles, learning guidance, local progress, and optional Apple service integration. It does not guarantee a particular cognitive or medical outcome.

2. Pro Lifetime

Pro includes every answer and all Pro features. One-time purchase. No subscription. Three sample answers per category are free to view again. Unlock Pro for all remaining answers. Existing answer-pack purchases can still be restored for their original difficulty.

3. Availability and fair play

CloudKit, Game Center, and the App Store can be unavailable. Local play remains available, while rankings or purchase changes may wait for Apple services. Manipulated scores may be excluded.

4. Your responsibility

Use the app lawfully and keep device backups appropriate to your needs. Features and bundled puzzles may improve in later versions without removing an already purchased entitlement.
