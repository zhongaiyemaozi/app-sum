隐私政策

轻扫描在设备本地完成 OCR 与增强；保存的文档可通过 Apple 私有 iCloud 容器同步，绝不会发送到开发者运营的服务器。

1. 我们处理的数据

你拍摄或导入的文档、OCR 文本、PDF 和缩略图会在设备本地用于扫描、整理和导出。

2. 数据保存位置

保存的 PDF、OCR 文字与扫描历史使用本地 Core Data 和 Apple 私有 iCloud 容器，便于在你的设备间保持可用；导出文件只会通过系统分享或保存到你选择的位置。

3. 我们不会收集什么

我们不运营开发者文档服务器，不读取无关文件，不接广告 SDK，不做跨 App 追踪。私有 iCloud 同步由 Apple 在你的账号下提供。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 买断由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们不会获得你的银行卡等支付信息。你通过邮件或 QQ 群联系支持时，仅会使用你主动选择的联系方式。

使用条款

请仅使用 轻扫描 处理你有权使用的内容和数据。

1. 一次性 Pro 买断

Pro 是名为 pro_lifetime 的非消耗型买断内购。购买后，在同一 Apple ID 登录的设备上可恢复 Pro 权益，具体以 App Store 可用性为准。

2. 合理使用

请仅扫描和分享你有权处理的文档。不要用本 App 伪造、篡改或传播违法材料。

3. 不作保证或专业建议

OCR 和图像增强可能出错。提交正式文件前，请自行核对文字、页序、清晰度和导出内容。

4. 更新与联系

我们可能为了质量、兼容性和合规要求更新 App 与条款。如有问题，可从设置页联系支持。

Privacy Policy

ScanLite performs OCR and enhancement on device. Saved documents can sync through Apple's private iCloud container and are never sent to a developer-operated server.

1. Data we process

Documents, OCR text, PDFs, and thumbnails you capture or import are processed on device for scanning, organization, and export.

2. Where data is stored

Saved PDFs, OCR text, and scan history use local Core Data and Apple's private iCloud container so they remain available on your devices. Exports only go through system sharing or locations you choose.

3. What we do not collect

We do not run a developer document server, read unrelated files, use ad SDKs, or perform cross-app tracking. Private iCloud sync is provided by Apple under your account.

4. Purchases and support

The lifetime Pro purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. If you email support or join the QQ group, that contact happens only through the app you choose.

Terms of Use

Please use ScanLite for content and data you have the right to process.

1. One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase named pro_lifetime. It unlocks the app's Pro limits on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, subject to App Store availability.

2. Acceptable use

Scan and share only documents you have rights to process. Do not use this app to forge, tamper with, or distribute unlawful materials.

3. No guarantees or professional advice

OCR and enhancement can be wrong. Before submitting official files, verify text, page order, clarity, and export contents yourself.

4. Changes and contact

We may update the app and these terms to improve quality, compatibility, and compliance. For questions, contact support from Settings.
