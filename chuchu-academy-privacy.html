初初学堂隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日。适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的初初学堂（原团团学堂）。

English

儿童隐私与本机数据

应用无需注册或登录，不含广告、分析或追踪 SDK。开发者不会收集儿童的个人信息。家长填写的孩子昵称以及学习进度、星星、复习记录和学习时长保存在设备上，用于展示课程进度和家长报告。这些资料不会发送给开发者，也不会用于广告或分析。

网络、购买与家长验证

应用使用 Apple 的 App 内购买和恢复购买服务，并查询 App Store 是否有新版本。Apple 处理付款，开发者不会收到付款资料。购买、外部链接和分享入口设有家长验证。家长主动分享或导出的内容，由家长选择接收方。

保存期限与删除

学习资料保存在应用的本机存储中。家长可在家长中心重置当前孩子的学习进度。通过系统的「删除 App」可移除该设备上的应用及其本地数据。

家长联系渠道

家长可通过 yemaozi2026@outlook.com 联系开发者，询问儿童隐私或请求删除支持邮件中的个人信息。开发者仅用家长主动发送的邮件地址和内容处理该请求。

Chuchu Academy Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy applies to Chuchu Academy (formerly Tuantuan Academy) on iPhone and iPad.

简体中文

Children’s privacy and on-device data

The app has no account, sign-in, ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect children’s personal information. A child’s nickname entered by a parent, learning progress, stars, review records, and study time are stored on the device to show progress and parent reports. The app does not send this information to the developer or use it for advertising or analytics.

Network use, purchases, and the parent gate

The app uses Apple’s In-App Purchase and restore services and checks the App Store for updates. Apple handles payment. The developer does not receive payment details. Purchases, external links, and sharing are behind a parent gate. Parents choose where to send anything they decide to share or export.

Retention and deletion

Learning records are stored in the app’s local storage. Parents can reset the current child’s learning progress in the Parent Center. Using Delete App removes the app and its local data from the device.

Contact for parents

Parents can email yemaozi2026@outlook.com with children’s privacy questions or to request deletion of personal information in support emails. The developer uses the email address and information parents choose to send only to handle their request.
