隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 18 日

MDQuill 尊重你的隐私。它以本地文档编辑为主，不提供账号、多人协作、广告或追踪。iCloud 同步是可选项，默认关闭。

我们处理的数据

你创建、导入、编辑、导出或通过分享进入的 Markdown、文本、HTML 和 PDF，保存在本机或你选择的系统文件位置。最近文档、导出格式、字数、阅读时长和编辑时间也保存在本机，用来支持最近列表。

可选的 iCloud 同步

在设置中打开 iCloud 同步后，Markdown 文档通过你自己的 iCloud Drive 私有容器同步到同一 Apple ID 的其他设备。数据留在你的 iCloud 账户里。开发者没有服务器，收不到文档，也不与第三方共享。同步在你开启前不会发生；关闭后停止继续同步。两台设备同时编辑时，保留较新的版本，另一份留在本机并标为冲突副本。

我们不会做的事

MDQuill 不把文档内容上传到开发者服务器，不出售个人信息，也不包含第三方广告追踪 SDK。

分享与删除

系统分享、导入和邮件反馈由系统完成，发送前由你确认。你可以在应用内删除本地记录，并用文件 App 管理文件。卸载应用通常会移除沙盒数据；应用外部的文件按系统文件规则保留。

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 18, 2026

MDQuill respects your privacy. It focuses on local document editing and does not provide accounts, collaboration, advertising, or tracking. iCloud sync is optional and off by default.

Data We Handle

Markdown, text, HTML, and PDF files you create, import, edit, export, or share into the app are stored locally on your device or in a system file location you choose. The app stores recent document records, export format, word count, reading time, creation time, open time, and edit time locally to support the recent list and editing experience.

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings, your Markdown documents are synced through your own private iCloud Drive container provided by Apple, so they are available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data stays within your personal iCloud account; we do not operate any server, do not receive your documents, and do not share them with third parties. Sync is off until you enable it, and turning it off stops further syncing. When a document is edited on two devices at once, the newer version is kept and the other is preserved locally as a conflict copy.

What We Do Not Do

MDQuill does not upload your document content to our servers (we have none), does not sell personal information, and does not include third-party advertising trackers.

Sharing and Deletion

System sharing, file import, and email feedback are handled by the system, and you confirm email before it is sent. You can delete local records in the app and manage files in the Files app. Uninstalling usually removes sandboxed data. Files saved outside the app follow the system.
