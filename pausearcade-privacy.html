隐私政策

摸鱼游戏厅以本地存储为主。除非你开启私有 iCloud 同步或自行分享，否则游戏数据留在设备上。

1. 我们处理的数据

App 会在设备本地处理游戏操作、成绩、设置、成就与存档。

2. 数据存储位置

进度保存在本地 Core Data。Pro 用户开启 iCloud 后，由 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库在自己的设备间同步。

3. 我们不收集的内容

我们不运营 App 账号服务器，不展示广告，不使用第三方分析，也不进行跨 App 或网站追踪。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 销售。我们不会收到支付卡信息。只有你主动选择时才会联系支持。

3. 游戏结果

Privacy Policy

Pause Arcade is local-first. Gameplay stays on your device unless you enable private iCloud sync or share something yourself.

1. Data we process

The app processes game input, scores, settings, achievements, and saved sessions on your device.

2. Where data is stored

Progress is stored in local Core Data. If a Pro user enables iCloud, Apple's private CloudKit database syncs progress across their devices.

3. What we do not collect

We do not run an app account server, show ads, use third-party analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

4. Purchases and support

Pro is sold by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive payment card details. Support contact occurs only when you choose it.

Play fairly, use the app lawfully, and take breaks that suit you.

2. Personal use

3. Game results

Scores, streaks, achievements, and generated challenges are entertainment features and have no cash value.

4. Changes and contact

We may update games and these terms for quality, compatibility, or compliance. Contact support from Settings with questions.
