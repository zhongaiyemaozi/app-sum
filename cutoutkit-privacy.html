隐私政策

抠图换背景 是本地优先工具。除非你主动启用 Apple iCloud 同步或系统分享，你的内容会留在设备本地。

1. 我们处理的数据

你选择或拍摄的照片会在设备本地用于抠图、换背景、商品图、缩略图和导出文件。

2. 数据保存位置

项目历史可保存到本地 Core Data，并可通过 Apple 私有 iCloud 容器同步；我们不自建服务器，也不会上传到我们的服务器。

3. 我们不会收集什么

我们不接广告 SDK，不做跨 App 追踪，不收集人脸特征，也不会用你的照片训练模型。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 买断由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们不会获得你的银行卡等支付信息。你通过邮件或 QQ 群联系支持时，仅会使用你主动选择的联系方式。

使用条款

请仅使用 抠图换背景 处理你有权使用的内容和数据。

1. 一次性 Pro 买断

Pro 是名为 pro_lifetime 的非消耗型买断内购。购买后，在同一 Apple ID 登录的设备上可恢复 Pro 权益，具体以 App Store 可用性为准。

2. 合理使用

请仅处理你拥有权利的图片。不要使用本 App 去除他人版权标识、制作欺骗性材料或侵犯他人肖像权。

3. 不作保证或专业建议

抠图换背景是用于移除背景和制作商品图的通用图片编辑工具。分享或发布前请检查每个导出结果。

4. 更新与联系

我们可能为了质量、兼容性和合规要求更新 App 与条款。如有问题，可从设置页联系支持。

Privacy Policy

CutoutKit is built as a local-first tool. Your work stays on your device unless you choose Apple iCloud sync or system sharing.

1. Data we process

Photos you choose or capture are processed on device for cutouts, background replacement, product images, thumbnails, and exports.

2. Where data is stored

Project history can be saved in local Core Data and synced through Apple's private iCloud container. We do not operate our own server or upload your images to us.

3. What we do not collect

We use no ad SDK, do no cross-app tracking, do not collect face attributes, and do not train models with your photos.

4. Purchases and support

The lifetime Pro purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. If you email support or join the QQ group, that contact happens only through the app you choose.

Terms of Use

Please use CutoutKit for content and data you have the right to process.

1. One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase named pro_lifetime. It unlocks the app's Pro limits on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, subject to App Store availability.

2. Acceptable use

Process only images you have rights to use. Do not use this app to remove others' copyright marks, create deceptive material, or violate portrait rights.

3. No guarantees or professional advice

CutoutKit is a general photo editing tool for background removal and product images. Review every export before sharing or publishing it.

4. Changes and contact

We may update the app and these terms to improve quality, compatibility, and compliance. For questions, contact support from Settings.
