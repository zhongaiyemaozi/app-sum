隐私政策

数学实验台在设备上计算。进度与实验存档可同步至你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，开发者无法查看。

1. 我们处理什么

输入的方程、表达式和三角形数值在本机计算。关卡进度、提示、用时和实验存档保存在本机，并在 iCloud 可用时同步至你的私有数据库。未完成关卡的操作快照仅存本机。

2. 我们不收集什么

没有账号，不需要登录；不接入广告和第三方统计 SDK；不追踪你在其他 App 或网站的行为；不申请相机、相册、定位、麦克风等权限。

3. 购买与联网

Pro 买断由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，我们拿不到你的付款信息。只有「更多 App」与新版本检查会访问 Apple 的公开接口；断网时其余功能照常使用。

4. 删除与联系

可在设置的外观与模型页面清除关卡进度，也可在我的实验中删除存档；这些删除会同步至 iCloud。卸载 App 仅移除本机数据，不会删除 iCloud 副本。可在系统 iCloud 存储设置中管理云端数据。疑问请从设置联系开发者。

Privacy Policy

MathBench calculates on your device. Progress and saved benches can sync through your private iCloud; the developer cannot access them.

1. What the app handles

Equations, expressions and triangle values are calculated on this device. Progress, hints, times and named benches are stored locally and sync to your private database when iCloud is available. Unfinished-level snapshots stay on this device.

2. What we don't collect

There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no third-party analytics SDK and no tracking across apps or websites. The app doesn't ask for the camera, photos, location or microphone.

3. Purchases and network

The Pro purchase is handled by Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details. Only More Apps and the update check contact Apple's public lookup service. Everything else works offline.

4. Deleting data and contact

Clear level progress in Settings > Appearance and model, or delete benches in My benches; these deletions sync to iCloud. Uninstalling removes local data only, not your iCloud copy. Manage cloud data in system iCloud storage settings. Contact the developer from Settings.
