隐私政策

生物实验台在设备上处理生物学习练习。本政策说明学习工具使用的信息。

1. 本地学习数据

关卡进度、提示、未完成练习和命名实验台参数保存在本机。序列输入在设备上解码，不发送至序列数据库。

2. 购买与外部服务

购买与恢复由 Apple 处理。商店查询、支持及社群入口使用时会连接相应服务，其适用各自政策。

3. 数据管理

可在设置→模型与进度清除关卡进度，在实验台列表删除存档。移除 App 会移除本地数据，请自行做好设备备份。

4. 隐私与联系

不包含广告或追踪 SDK，不申请相机、位置或健康权限。隐私问题可通过设置→支持与社区联系我们。

使用条款

用生物实验台练习并理解学科知识。

1. 教学范围

本 App 演示简化的生物概率、编码规则及种群模型。结果仅作学习参考，不作为临床、育种或实验室操作指引。

2. 购买权益

Pro 为可选的一次性非消耗型购买，由 Apple 管理付款并使用购买账户恢复。速算结果无需 Pro 仍可查看。

3. 合理使用

请使用合成教学输入，并核对模型假设、单位及课程要求。不使用简化模型作人类性状、亲缘或临床风险决策。

4. 数据与支持

请备份设备中的学习数据。Apple 及其他外部服务的可用性不受本 App 控制；问题或更正可在设置中联系支持。

Privacy policy

BioBench processes biology exercises on your device. This policy explains the information used by its learning tools.

1. Local learning data

Level progress, hints, unfinished attempts and named bench parameters are stored locally. Sequence inputs are decoded on your device and are not sent to a sequence database.

2. Purchases and external services

Apple processes purchases and restoration. App Store lookup, support and community links contact their respective services when used; those services apply their own policies.

3. Data choices

Clear level progress in Settings → Models & progress. Named bench saves can be deleted in the bench list. Removing the app removes local data; maintain your own device backups.

4. Privacy and contact

No advertising or tracking SDK is included. BioBench does not request camera, location or health permissions. Contact us through Settings → Support & community for privacy questions.

Terms of use

Use BioBench to practise and understand school subjects.

1. Educational scope

BioBench demonstrates simplified biological probabilities, coding rules and population models. Results are learning references, not clinical, breeding or laboratory instructions.

2. Purchases

Pro is an optional non-consumable, one-time purchase. Apple manages payment and restoration through the purchasing Apple Account. Free calculator results remain available without Pro.

3. Responsible use

Use synthetic teaching inputs. Check assumptions, units and course requirements. Do not use these simplified models to make decisions about human traits, relationships or clinical risks.

4. Data and support

Keep device backups for saved learning data. Availability of Apple and other external services is outside our control. Contact support from Settings for issues and corrections.
