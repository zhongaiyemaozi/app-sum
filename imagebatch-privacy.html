隐私政策

图片批量助手是本地优先工具。除非你主动启用 Apple iCloud 同步或系统分享，你的内容会留在设备本地。

1. 我们处理的数据

你选择的图片会在设备本地用于压缩、裁剪、改尺寸、格式转换、去 EXIF/GPS 和批量导出。

2. 数据保存位置

批处理历史保存在本地 Core Data，并可通过 Apple 私有 iCloud 容器同步；输出文件由你主动保存或分享。

3. 我们不会收集什么

我们不上传原图或结果图，不保留定位元数据，不接广告 SDK，不做跨 App 追踪。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 买断由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们不会获得你的银行卡等支付信息。你通过邮件或 QQ 群联系支持时，仅会使用你主动选择的联系方式。

使用条款

请仅使用图片批量助手处理你有权使用的内容和数据。

1. 一次性 Pro 买断

Pro 是名为 pro_lifetime 的非消耗型买断内购。购买后，在同一 Apple ID 登录的设备上可恢复 Pro 权益，具体以 App Store 可用性为准。

2. 合理使用

请仅处理你有权使用的图片。去除 EXIF/GPS 是隐私工具，不应用于规避法律责任或误导他人。

3. 不作保证或专业建议

导出结果受原始素材质量、系统能力和设备性能影响。正式发布或商用前请自行检查结果。

4. 更新与联系

我们可能为了质量、兼容性和合规要求更新 App 与条款。如有问题，可从设置页联系支持。

Privacy Policy

ImageBatch is built as a local-first tool. Your work stays on your device unless you choose Apple iCloud sync or system sharing.

1. Data we process

Photos you select are processed on device for compression, crop, resizing, conversion, EXIF/GPS removal, and batch export.

2. Where data is stored

Batch history is stored in local Core Data and can sync through Apple's private iCloud container. Output files are saved or shared only when you choose.

3. What we do not collect

We do not upload source or result images, retain location metadata, use ad SDKs, or perform cross-app tracking.

4. Purchases and support

The lifetime Pro purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. If you email support or join the QQ group, that contact happens only through the app you choose.

Terms of Use

Please use ImageBatch for content and data you have the right to process.

1. One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase named pro_lifetime. It unlocks the app's Pro limits on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, subject to App Store availability.

2. Acceptable use

Process only images you have rights to use. EXIF/GPS removal is a privacy tool and should not be used to evade legal responsibility or mislead others.

3. No guarantees or professional advice

Export results depend on source quality, system capabilities, and device performance. Check results yourself before publishing or commercial use.

4. Changes and contact

We may update the app and these terms to improve quality, compatibility, and compliance. For questions, contact support from Settings.
