隐私政策

WallMark 以本地优先。除非你开启 Apple iCloud 同步或自行分享文件，房间规划都留在本机。

1. 我们处理的数据

WallMark 会在设备本地处理用于 AR 测量/标记/扫描的相机画面、磁场参考扫描的磁力计数据，以及你保存的项目备注与照片。

2. 数据存储位置

项目、标记、测量、历史与报告元数据保存在本地 Core Data。开启 Pro 的 iCloud 同步后，会通过 Apple CloudKit 私有库在你的设备间同步。

3. 我们不收集的内容

我们不运营自建服务器，不把房间照片上传到开发者后端，不使用广告 SDK，也不做跨 App 追踪。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 终身版由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 销售。我们不会收到你的支付卡信息。支持邮箱或 Telegram 联系仅发生在你选择的应用中。

使用条款

请将 WallMark 作为规划辅助，而非专业检测设备。

1. 一次性 Pro 购买

Pro 是非消耗型终身买断（pro_lifetime）。在同一 Apple ID 登录且 App Store 可用的设备上解锁 WallMark Pro 权益。

2. 可接受使用

请仅记录你有权记录的空间与照片。不要把本 App 当作打孔前的唯一安全依据。

3. 无保证与非专业建议

AR、规划洞察、磁场与水管风险结果均为参考辅助，可能有误。施工或打孔前请用专业工具确认。

4. 变更与联系

我们可能为质量、兼容与合规更新应用与条款。如有问题请在设置中联系支持。

Privacy Policy

WallMark is local-first. Your room plans stay on your device unless you enable Apple iCloud sync or share a file yourself.

1. Data we process

WallMark processes camera frames for AR measuring/marking/scanning, magnetometer samples for magnetic reference scans, and project notes/photos you save. Processing runs on device.

2. Where data is stored

Projects, markers, measurements, history, and report metadata are stored in local Core Data. When Pro iCloud sync is enabled, Apple CloudKit private database syncs your data across your devices.

3. What we do not collect

We do not operate an app server, do not upload room photos to a developer backend, do not use ad SDKs, and do not perform cross-app tracking.

4. Purchases and support

Pro Lifetime is sold by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your payment card details. Support email or Telegram contact happens only in the apps you choose.

Terms of Use

Please use WallMark as a planning aid, not as professional inspection equipment.

1. One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase (pro_lifetime). It unlocks WallMark Pro limits on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, subject to App Store availability.

2. Acceptable use

Use WallMark only for spaces and photos you are allowed to document. Do not rely on the app as a sole safety authority before drilling.

3. No guarantees or professional advice

AR, planning insights, magnetic, and pipe-risk outputs are reference aids. They can be wrong. Verify with professional tools before drilling or construction decisions.

4. Changes and contact

We may update the app and these terms for quality, compatibility, and compliance. Contact support from Settings for questions.
