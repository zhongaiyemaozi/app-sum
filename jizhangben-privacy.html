1. 总则 1.1 极简记账本 重视你的个人信息与账本数据安全。 1.2 本政策说明你在使用本地记账、iCloud 备份、数据导出等功能时的数据处理规则。 1.2.1 若本政策与适用法律法规或 Apple 平台规则存在冲突，以法律法规和平台规则为准。

2. 我们不收集什么 2.1 本应用没有账号体系，不要求注册或登录，也不收集手机号、邮箱等身份信息。 2.2 极简记账本 不自建业务服务器保存你的账本内容，不读取你的账单用于广告、画像或对外出售。 2.2.1 应用内不含第三方广告或行为追踪 SDK。

3. 数据保存与备份 3.1 你录入的账单、分类、账户等数据默认保存在当前设备本地。 3.2 专业版可将账本快照备份到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库；该数据归属于你的 Apple 账号，开发者无法读取。解锁专业版且设备已登录 iCloud 后，默认在账本改动后自动备份，你可以在「设置 › iCloud 备份 › 自动备份」中关闭。 3.2.1 首次安装或重装后，若本机没有账单且已解锁专业版，应用会尝试从你的 iCloud 备份中恢复。 3.3 如设备未登录 iCloud、网络不可用或 Apple 服务异常，账本仍可继续本地使用。

4. 截图记账与相册 4.1 截图记账使用系统内置的文字识别能力，在你的设备本地完成，图片与识别文本都不会上传。 4.2 仅当你主动开启「识别后删除相册原图」时，应用才会请求相册写入权限并尝试删除你所选的那张照片，删除前系统会再次要求你确认。 4.3 若你在「截图记账」设置中开启「打开 App 时检测最近截图」，应用会在前台打开或回到前台时读取相册里最近 10 分钟内最新的一张截图，并询问你是否记一笔。该功能默认关闭，不会在后台扫描，也不会自动入账。

5. 你的控制权 5.1 专业版可在设置中查看 iCloud 状态，并执行备份、恢复、删除云端备份。 5.1.1 删除 iCloud 备份不会删除当前设备上的本地账本。 5.2 你可以随时导出全部账单，或在设置中清空本机所有数据。 5.3 卸载应用即会删除本机账本；如需同时清理云端，请先删除 iCloud 备份。

6. 风险与说明 6.1 iCloud、网络、系统限制或 Apple 服务异常，可能导致备份延迟、失败或暂时不可用。 6.2 极简记账本 会尽量提供明确的状态提示，但不承诺绝对实时或绝对无误的备份结果。

1. Overview 1.1 Expense Tracker takes the security of your personal information and financial records seriously. 1.2 This policy explains how data is handled when you use local tracking, iCloud backup and data export. 1.2.1 Where this policy conflicts with applicable law or Apple platform rules, those take precedence.

2. What we do not collect 2.1 This app has no account system. It never asks you to register or sign in, and does not collect your phone number, email or other identifying information. 2.2 Expense Tracker operates no application server that stores your records, and never reads your records for advertising, profiling or resale. 2.2.1 The app contains no third-party advertising or behavioural tracking SDKs.

3. Storage and backup 3.1 Records, categories and accounts you enter are stored locally on your device by default. 3.2 Pro can write a snapshot to your own private iCloud database. That data belongs to your Apple account and the developer cannot read it. After Pro is unlocked and the device is signed in to iCloud, backups run automatically after changes by default; you can switch to manual-only in Settings › iCloud backup › Automatic backup. 3.2.1 After a fresh install, if this device has no records and Pro is unlocked, the app attempts to restore from your iCloud backup. 3.3 If iCloud is unavailable or the network fails, local tracking continues to work.

4. Receipt scanning and your photo library 4.1 Receipt scanning uses the operating system's on-device text recognition. Neither the image nor the recognised text is uploaded. 4.2 Only if you explicitly enable "Delete the photo after scanning" will the app request photo library write access and attempt to remove the photo you selected — and iOS asks you to confirm before deleting. 4.3 If you turn on "Check for a recent screenshot when opening the app" in Receipt scanning settings, the app — only while in the foreground — reads the latest screenshot from the last 10 minutes and asks whether to record it. This is off by default; the app never scans in the background and never saves silently.

5. Your controls 5.1 With Pro you can check iCloud status in Settings and back up, restore or delete the cloud backup. 5.1.1 Deleting the iCloud backup does not delete the ledger on this device. 5.2 You can export all records at any time, or erase all local data from Settings. 5.3 Deleting the app removes the local ledger. To clear the cloud copy too, delete the iCloud backup first.

6. Risks and limitations 6.1 iCloud, network conditions, system restrictions or Apple service issues may delay backups or make them temporarily unavailable. 6.2 Expense Tracker aims to surface clear status information but cannot guarantee instantaneous or error-free backups.
