ChemBench Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

ChemBench does not require an account, does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and does not send personal data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

ChemBench stores settings, learning progress, completion records, and experiments on your device so the app can remember your work. You can remove local records from Settings.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit may synchronize progress and experiment records through your private iCloud database. The developer cannot access this private database. The app remains usable with local storage if iCloud is unavailable.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit processes the optional Pro Lifetime purchase and restoration. ChemBench does not receive or store your payment details.

Data collection and tracking

ChemBench does not collect data for advertising, analytics, or tracking and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com.

ChemBench 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日

ChemBench 无需注册账号，不含广告或第三方分析，不会向开发者发送个人数据。

设备上的数据

应用会在设备上保存设置、学习进度、完成记录和实验记录，以便继续使用。你可以在“设置”中删除本机记录。

私人 iCloud 同步

iCloud 可用时，Apple CloudKit 可能通过你的私人 iCloud 数据库同步进度和实验记录。开发者无法访问该私人数据库。iCloud 不可用时，应用仍可使用本地存储。

购买

可选的 Pro 终身买断和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。ChemBench 不会接收或保存你的付款资料。

数据收集与跟踪

ChemBench 不会为广告、分析或跟踪收集数据，也不包含第三方广告或分析 SDK。

联系

如有隐私或支持问题，请发送邮件至 zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com。
