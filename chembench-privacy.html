隐私政策

化学实验台以本机为先。除非你打开私人 iCloud 同步，关卡和计算都留在设备上。

1. 设备上的数据

关卡、进度、未完成的实验台、保存的实验、计算和偏好都留在设备上，因此全部功能可离线使用。

2. 私人 iCloud 同步

iCloud 可用时，Core Data 可能把进度镜像到你的私人 CloudKit。开发者收不到这些数据；同步失败时本机仍可玩。

3. 购买

通过 Apple StoreKit 验证和恢复 Pro 及历史答案包购买。不含广告或第三方统计 SDK。

4. 控制与支持

无需账号即可游玩，也可在设置里删除本机记录。对本政策有疑问请联系支持。

用它练化学计算。它不是实验操作手册，也不是教材替代品。

1. 教学模型

化学实验台提供离线关卡、反应卡、速算工具和可选的计算沙盒，不包含任何实验操作步骤。模型经过简化，不保证课程成绩、考试结果或认证。

2. Pro 终身版

Pro 包含全部答案与所有 Pro 功能。一次买断，无订阅。 每种类型有 3 道免费答案样题，可反复查看；其余答案需开启 Pro。 已购买的旧答案包仍可恢复原难度的答案权益。

3. 可用性

CloudKit 和 App Store 可能不可用。本机游玩仍可用，购买状态变化可能要等 Apple 服务。

4. 你的责任

请合法使用，并按自己的需要备份设备。功能和内置关卡以后可能改进，但不会拿走已购权益。

Privacy Policy

ChemBench is local-first. Your levels and calculations stay on your device unless you enable private iCloud sync.

1. Data on your device

Levels, progress, unfinished benches, saved setups, calculations, and preferences stay on device so everything works offline.

2. Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, Core Data may mirror progress to your private CloudKit database. The developer does not receive this data, and local play remains if sync fails.

3. Purchases

Apple StoreKit verifies and restores Pro and previously purchased answer packs. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is included.

4. Control and support

You can play without an account and delete local records from Settings. Contact support for questions about this policy.

Terms of Use

Use the app to practise chemistry calculations. It is not a lab manual and not a textbook substitute.

1. Teaching models

ChemBench provides offline levels, reaction cards, quick calculators, and optional calculation sandboxes. It contains no experimental procedures. Models are simplified and do not guarantee a course outcome, exam result or certification.

2. Pro Lifetime

Pro includes every answer and all Pro features. One-time purchase. No subscription. Three sample answers per category are free to view again. Unlock Pro for all remaining answers. Existing answer-pack purchases can still be restored for their original difficulty.

3. Availability

CloudKit and the App Store can be unavailable. Local play remains available while purchase changes may wait for Apple services.

4. Your responsibility

Use the app lawfully and keep device backups that suit you. Features and bundled levels may improve later without removing an already purchased entitlement.
