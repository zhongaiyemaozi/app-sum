隐私政策

习惯周报 是本地优先工具。除非你主动启用 Apple iCloud 同步或系统分享，你的内容会留在设备本地。

1. 我们处理的数据

你创建的习惯、打卡记录、提醒设置和周报缓存会用于展示今日进度、连续天数与报告。

2. 数据保存位置

习惯数据保存在本地 Core Data，并可通过 Apple 私有 iCloud 容器同步。HealthKit 数据只读获取，用于趋势展示，不写入我们的数据库。

3. 我们不会收集什么

我们不上传健康数据，不做医疗诊断、治疗建议或疾病预测，也不接广告和跨 App 追踪。

4. 购买与支持

Pro 买断由 Apple StoreKit 处理，我们不会获得你的银行卡等支付信息。你通过邮件或 QQ 群联系支持时，仅会使用你主动选择的联系方式。

使用条款

请仅使用 习惯周报 处理你有权使用的内容和数据。

1. 一次性 Pro 买断

Pro 是名为 pro_lifetime 的非消耗型买断内购。购买后，在同一 Apple ID 登录的设备上可恢复 Pro 权益，具体以 App Store 可用性为准。

2. 合理使用

本 App 仅用于个人记录和趋势复盘。请勿把周报当作医疗结论，也不要用它替代医生或专业健康服务。

3. 不作保证或专业建议

健康数据可能因设备、授权和 Apple Health 数据源不同而变化。本 App 不提供诊断、治疗、疾病预测或紧急提醒。

4. 更新与联系

我们可能为了质量、兼容性和合规要求更新 App 与条款。如有问题，可从设置页联系支持。

Privacy Policy

HabitPulse is built as a local-first tool. Your work stays on your device unless you choose Apple iCloud sync or system sharing.

1. Data we process

Habits, check-ins, reminder settings, and report cache are used to show today's progress, streaks, and reports.

2. Where data is stored

Habit data is stored in local Core Data and can sync through Apple's private iCloud container. HealthKit data is read-only for trends and is not written to our database.

3. What we do not collect

We do not upload health data, provide diagnosis, treatment advice, or disease prediction, and we use no ads or cross-app tracking.

4. Purchases and support

The lifetime Pro purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. If you email support or join the QQ group, that contact happens only through the app you choose.

Terms of Use

Please use HabitPulse for content and data you have the right to process.

1. One-time Pro purchase

Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase named pro_lifetime. It unlocks the app's Pro limits on devices signed in with the same Apple ID, subject to App Store availability.

2. Acceptable use

This app is for personal tracking and trend review only. Do not treat reports as medical conclusions or replacements for doctors or professional health services.

3. No guarantees or professional advice

Health data may vary by device, permissions, and Apple Health sources. This app provides no diagnosis, treatment, disease prediction, or emergency alerting.

4. Changes and contact

We may update the app and these terms to improve quality, compatibility, and compliance. For questions, contact support from Settings.
