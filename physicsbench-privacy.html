PhysicsBench Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

PhysicsBench does not require an account. It has no ads or third-party analytics and does not send personal data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings, progress, unfinished benches, saved experiments, imported or created levels, and Pencil notes on your device. Principle Card narration uses an installed system voice and does not use the microphone or send the text to a server.

AR camera and sharing

The camera runs while you use AR placement to find a surface and show the model on it. The 3D viewer works without camera access. Tapping Capture Frame creates a temporary image on your device. In AR, that image can include your surroundings. The app shares a card only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. Screen recording is a separate feature you start yourself.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit can sync progress and experiment records through your private iCloud database. The developer cannot access this database. Local storage is available without iCloud.

Purchases and support

Apple StoreKit handles the optional Pro Lifetime purchase and purchase restoration. The app does not receive your payment details. For support or privacy questions, email zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com.

物理实验台隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 11 日

物理实验台无需注册账号，不含广告或第三方分析，也不会向开发者发送个人数据。

保存在设备上的数据

应用在本机保存设置、学习进度、未完成的实验、已保存的实验、导入或自建的关卡及 Pencil 笔记。原理卡朗读使用已安装的系统语音，不使用麦克风，也不会把文本发送到服务器。

AR 相机和分享

使用 AR 摆放时，相机用于识别平面并显示模型。3D 预览无需相机权限。点击「截取画面」会在本机暂存一张图片。AR 截图可能包含周围环境。只有你在系统分享面板选择目的地后才会分享卡片。实验台录屏由你另行启动。

私人 iCloud 同步

iCloud 可用时，Apple CloudKit 可通过你的私人 iCloud 数据库同步进度和实验记录。开发者无法访问该数据库。iCloud 不可用时可以继续使用本地存储。

购买和支持

可选的 Pro 终身版买断及恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理，应用不会收到你的付款资料。如有支持或隐私问题，请发送邮件至 zhongaiyemaozi@outlook.com。
