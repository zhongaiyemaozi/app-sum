隐私政策

右键菜单在你的 Mac 上处理你在访达里选中的文件，例如新建文件、复制路径、压缩、解压和批量重命名。

1. 处理范围

文件内容只在本机使用。应用不把文件上传到开发者服务器，没有账号，也没有广告或追踪 SDK。

2. 云盘目录

iCloud 云盘或 OneDrive 里的文件仍是你已经放在那些服务里的文件。右键菜单不会另做一份上传给开发者。

3. 购买

Pro 是一次性买断，由 Apple StoreKit 在本机校验和恢复。开发者收不到银行卡信息。

Privacy policy

Falcon Menu works on the files you select in Finder on your Mac, including new files, copying a path, archive, extract, and batch rename.

1. Scope

File contents are used on this Mac. The app does not upload them to a developer server. There is no account and no advertising or tracking SDK.

2. Cloud folders

Files already in iCloud Drive or OneDrive stay in those services. Falcon Menu does not upload a separate copy to the developer.

3. Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase, checked and restored by Apple StoreKit on the device. The developer does not receive card details.
